## D. Things I'd leave out, and why

- **"Shelly $225K → $280K"**: conflicts with the intake ($225K → $435K). ⚠️ **Please confirm the right number with Tamara before anything ships.** The deck currently uses $435K.
- **"64% of women never negotiate" and "women who prepare are 74% more likely to get a raise"**: no sources. The 64% also conflicts with the intake's "60%+". I'd keep the placeholder until we have a citation.
- **"Proven system… negotiate 20–30% more" and "tested by hundreds of women"**: unsubstantiated claims for an Affluent audience. Only keep them if Tamara can back them up.
- **Old offer (six weeks, $3,997, "today only $997", "but wait, there's more", value stack of $8,685)**: conflicts with the intake (8 weeks) and is the manipulative scarcity she says she wants to avoid. The **bonus names** (The Win-Win Playbook, The Receipts Dossier, The Courage Code) could fill our bonus placeholder if they still exist, with values confirmed.
- **"Workbook, tip sheets, scripts" and "knowing exactly what to say"**: tactics, which are reserved for paying clients.
- **"3 mistakes" section (not negotiating, not preparing, letting emotions lead)**: leans how-to. The one line in #7 is worth keeping.
- **"Two free gifts for staying to the end"**: a good retention device, but the gifts aren't defined in the deck. Add it only if you have a real gift to give (e.g. the Courage to Ask Blueprint lead magnet).
- **Frequency and subconscious-mind language (v4 slide 3)**: she wants "just a hint" of spiritual. I'd keep it to the presenter notes at most.
